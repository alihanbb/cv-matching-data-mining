Data Scientist

Description:
We are hiring a data scientist to design machine learning and NLP models that
power our recommendation and risk scoring products.

Responsibilities:
- Build classification and ranking models with scikit-learn or PyTorch.
- Design experiment and evaluation protocols with offline metrics.
- Work with engineers to deploy models in production.

Requirements:
- Minimum 3 years of experience in machine learning or data science.
- Strong Python skills with pandas, NumPy, and scikit-learn.
- SQL knowledge for working with large datasets.
- Familiarity with NLP and text mining techniques.

Nice to have:
- Experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Cloud experience on AWS or GCP.
- Familiarity with Spark or distributed data tools.
